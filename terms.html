What Shyaka is

Shyaka is a wardrobe and outfit-planning app for the iPhone. You keep the clothes you own, build and plan looks, and see what you wore. It also does three other things:

  • Shop pieces. You can browse pieces from local shops. Buying always happens on the shop's own website. Shyaka has no checkout and takes no payment for clothes.
  • Following. You can follow other people and share looks with the people who follow you: one-way follow, private by default, with likes and polls. It may be switched on for some people before others.
  • Personal listings. People may sell clothes they own, with a seven-day paid listing. This is switched off for now. The rules below apply when it opens.

Using Shyaka

You must be 13 or older to use Shyaka, and 18 or older to sell. Keep your sign-in details to yourself and tell us if you think someone else has used your account.

Your content

Photos, looks, posts and listings you add stay yours. You give us permission to store them and show them to the people you choose, only so the app can work. Your closet is private to you. Looks you post are shown to the people who follow you, or to everyone if you switch your account to public. Do not add content you do not have the right to use, and do not add other people's photos without their permission.

Following other people

Following is one way: you can follow someone without them following you back. Every account starts private, so a person approves each follower. You can switch to a public account in Settings. Followers can like a look and vote in a poll. There are no comments, stories or messages between people in Shyaka.

Be respectful. Do not harass, threaten, spam or pretend to be someone else. You can unfollow someone, remove a follower, block a person, and report a look or an account from the app.

Shops and the pieces they show

The pieces in Market and Shops belong to the shops that show them. Each shop is responsible for its pieces: the description, photos, price, stock, quality, delivery, returns and any complaint about them. When you tap Buy, you leave Shyaka and deal with the shop on its own website, under its own terms. Shyaka does not sell, ship, take payment for, or take back clothes from shops, and does not guarantee any shop, piece or price. You can save a shop piece's photo to your own private closet to plan outfits with it.

No messages inside Shyaka

Shyaka does not carry messages between people. If a seller publishes contact details on a listing, you use those details outside the app, and you deal with them at your own risk.

Zero tolerance

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, scams and anything illegal are not allowed. We review reports, normally within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules and block the accounts that post it. You can report and block people, looks and listings from the app.

What you may not sell

Personal listings are for clothes, shoes and accessories you own. You may not list:

  • weapons, or anything made to look like one;
  • used underwear or used swimwear;
  • medicines, and cosmetics or toiletries that have been opened;
  • counterfeit or replica goods, or anything that copies another brand's trademark;
  • stolen goods;
  • goods that have been recalled for safety;
  • anything else that is illegal to sell in Egypt.

When you sell

Personal listings are switched off for now. When they open: by publishing a listing you confirm that you own the item and have the right to sell it, and that the photos and description are accurate and honest about its condition. Listings are reviewed before they go live. Publication costs a fee for seven days, paid through Apple in-app purchase. The fee pays for showing the listing, not for the clothes.

Shyaka is not a shop: there is no checkout, shipping, returns or payout. Buyers and sellers deal with each other directly, at their own risk. Meet in a public place, check items before paying, and never send money in advance. We do not guarantee any sale, buyer, seller or item.

When we remove a listing

We may remove any listing that breaks these terms, and we do not refund the fee for a listing removed for breaking them. Apple's own refund rules still apply. If we remove a listing by mistake, we will put it back or give you a free re-listing for the days it had left.

Include a link to or screenshot of what you are reporting, what you own, and how to reach you. We will review it and remove what infringes.
