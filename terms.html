What Shyaka is

Shyaka is a wardrobe and outfit-planning app for the iPhone. You keep the clothes you own, build and plan looks, and see what you wore. You can also browse pieces from local shops. Buying always happens on the shop's own website. Shyaka has no checkout and takes no payment for clothes.

You can also list a piece of your own for sale (a personal listing, see below). Following other people is switched off for now. We will update these terms before it opens to everyone.
