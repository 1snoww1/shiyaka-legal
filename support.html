Support

The quickest way to reach us is inside the app: You, then Settings, then Help and feedback. You can attach basic app information (version, platform) if you want us to see it.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Settings, then Data and account, then Delete account. You can export your data from the same place first.
Can I try the app without signing up?
Yes. Choose "Explore the demo" on the sign-in screen. It uses a sample wardrobe and nothing is sent anywhere.
How do I report a post, person or listing?
Open it, then choose Report and pick a reason. We aim to review reports within 48 hours.
TestFlight issues
Use the screenshot feedback option in the TestFlight app, or Help and feedback in Shiyaka.
