Support

Reach us inside the app: menu, then Settings, then Help & feedback. You can also email support@shyaka.art or message us on WhatsApp at +20 106 091 7193. Please never send passwords or card details.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Menu, then Settings. Under Your data, tap Delete account. You can tap Export my data in the same place first to get a copy.
Can I try the app without signing up?
Yes. Tap "Just look around" on the sign-in screen. It opens a sample wardrobe, marked Demo, and nothing is sent anywhere.
How do I add my clothes?
Tap the + in the bottom bar, then Add a piece (or Add clothes in the menu), and take or choose a photo. Your clothes are private and appear in My closet, which you can open from the menu or from Home.
How do I buy a piece I see in Shyaka?
Open Market (or Shops in the menu), open a piece and tap Buy. That takes you to the shop's own website, where you pay and the shop delivers. Shyaka has no checkout and takes no payment for clothes. Tap Add to my closet to plan outfits with a piece before you decide.
How does following work?
You follow someone from their profile, which you can find with Search. Following is one way, like Instagram. Accounts are private at first, so a person approves each follower; you can make yours public in Settings. Followers can like a look and vote in a poll. There are no comments or messages. Following may not be open to everyone yet.
How do I sell something?
Selling between people is switched off for now. When it opens you must be 18 or older, and you will add photos, details, a price and a phone number. Listings are reviewed first; then you pay a fee through Apple to publish it for seven days. Renew it by hand when it ends, and mark it sold once it has sold.
How do I stay safe when buying or selling?
Meet in a busy public place, check the item before paying, and never send money in advance. If something feels wrong, walk away and report the listing or person.
How do I report or block someone?
Open the look, account or listing, tap its menu or Report, and pick a reason. On a profile you can also block the person or remove them as a follower. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.
