Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Settings, then Show advanced, then Delete account. You can export your data from the same place first.
Can I try the app without signing up?
Yes. Choose "Explore the demo" on the sign-in screen. It uses a sample wardrobe and nothing is sent anywhere.
How do I sell something?
You must be 18 or older. Open Market, tap Sell and add photos, details, a price and a phone number. Listings are reviewed first; then you pay a fee through Apple to publish it for seven days. Renew it by hand when it ends, and tap Mark sold once it has sold.
How do I stay safe when buying or selling?
Meet in a busy public place, check the item before paying, and never send money in advance. If something feels wrong, walk away and report the listing or person.
How do I report a post, person or listing?
Open it, then choose Report and pick a reason. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.
