Get Shyaka for iPhone

Finish on your iPhone

  1. Install Shyaka on your iPhone and open it.
  2. On the sign-in screen, under “For business”, tap Continue with Shopify. If you already use Shyaka, sign in and go to Business, then Connect with Shopify.
  3. Choose this shop. Shopify may ask you to sign in, but you don’t need to approve Shyaka again.
  4. Your business is set up from the shop and its active products are imported. Choose which ones appear in the Market.

Disconnect

Uninstall Shyaka in your Shopify admin: Settings, then Apps, then Shyaka, then Uninstall. We then delete the connection and every product we imported. Shyaka only reads your products; it never sees orders, customers or payments.
