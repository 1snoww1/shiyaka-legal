Credits

Built from the scanner credits list on 2026-10-10.

Shiyaka's scanner recognises clothes on your phone. Thank you to the people and projects whose work made it possible.

Models in the app

  • Shiyaka's own models (what a piece is, and cutting out pieces that are laid flat or hung), trained in October 2026 on the collections and photos below.
  • U²-Net and U²-NetP (Qin et al.), Apache License 2.0: cutting a piece out of its background.
  • ISNet (Qin et al.), Apache License 2.0: detailed cutouts.
  • SCHP (Self-Correction for Human Parsing, Li et al.), MIT Licence: separating clothes from the person wearing them.

Open-source software libraries are listed in the app under Settings → Privacy & Terms → Open-source acknowledgements. Weather data: MET Norway, CC BY 4.0.

Shiyaka's own scanner models ("what is it" and the clothes-only cutout, trained October 2026) learned from the collections and photos below.
