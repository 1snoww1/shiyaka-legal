Shyaka's scanner recognises clothes on your phone. Thank you to the people and projects whose work made it possible.

Models in the app

  • Shyaka's own models (what a piece is, and cutting out pieces that are laid flat or hung), trained in October and November 2026 on the collections and photos below.
  • U²-Net and U²-NetP (Qin et al.), Apache License 2.0: cutting a piece out of its background.
  • ISNet (Qin et al.), Apache License 2.0: detailed cutouts.
  • BiRefNet (Zheng et al.), MIT License: cleaner cutouts of pieces nobody is wearing, run on Replicate when you allow clothing recognition.
  • SAM 3 (Segment Anything with Concepts, Meta), SAM License: cutting out the one piece you tap in a photo of someone wearing it, run on Replicate when you allow clothing recognition.
  • SCHP (Self-Correction for Human Parsing, Li et al.), MIT Licence: separating clothes from the person wearing them.

Open-source software libraries are listed in the app under Settings → Privacy & Terms → Open-source acknowledgements. Weather data: MET Norway, CC BY 4.0. Website font: Inter (The Inter Project Authors), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
