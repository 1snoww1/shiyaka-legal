For shops

Be in the closet she dresses from.

Shyaka is an iPhone app that tells women in Egypt what to wear today from the clothes they already own, with a Market of pieces from local shops. Your pieces sit one tap from the closet they dress from, and buying always happens on your own site.

Email us

Who uses it

  • Women in Egypt, 18 to 35, with an iPhone, who follow local brands.
  • Modest dressing is part of the idea from day one, not an extra.
  • They add their own clothes by photo, get a look for the day and plan the week. They save your pieces, add one to their closet to plan outfits around it, and tap Buy, which opens your own site.

Founding shops

Shops that join now are founding shops. A founding shop gets:

  • The Growth plan free until 30 April 2027: up to 150 products in Shyaka. Your shop page in the app calls it “Founding”.
  • Your logo on shyaka.art.
  • A say in what we build. You tell us what is missing, and we listen first.

In return we ask for honest feedback and your logo on our site. Nothing else.

What you will see

Four numbers for each product and for the whole shop, over the last 7 or 30 days:

  1. Views: how many people opened your piece.
  2. Saved: how many saved it.
  3. Closet adds: how many added your piece to their closet to plan outfits with it.
  4. Buy taps: how many tapped Buy and went to your site.

Your own team is never counted. You see totals, never names.

Ways to connect

One approval from you, and you choose which products show.

  1. WooCommerce. Install Shyaka for WooCommerce from the WordPress plugin directory, press Approve, then tick “Show in Shyaka” on each product. See Shyaka for WooCommerce.
  2. Shopify (coming soon). The Shyaka app is with Shopify for review. Once it is listed, you install it from the Shopify App Store and pick the products on Shyaka’s page in your Shopify admin. Want an invitation sooner? Ask us. See Shyaka for Shopify.
  3. On Instagram, Salla, Zid, Wix or your own site? Tell us which. For now, products come into Shyaka only through WooCommerce or Shopify, and we will write when yours is ready.

What we never do

  • We never take payments. The customer pays you, on your site.
  • We never ship anything. You send the order as you do today.
  • We never sell or hand over customer data. You see totals, not names.
