Shiyaka

Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-08 · Draft for the TestFlight beta. This text has not had a lawyer's review.

Shiyaka is an iPhone app for keeping a wardrobe, building looks, planning what to wear and sharing looks with friends. This page explains what the app stores about you and why. It is written from what the app actually does, not from a template.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, and your name if you give it or if Apple or Google shares it when you sign in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never readable by us.
  • Your wardrobe. Photos of your clothes, profile photo, looks, plans, wear history, collections and trips. These are private to you unless you choose to share a look.
  • Things you share. Posts, questions, stories, comments and votes you publish to friends you have accepted.
  • Classified listings. If you publish a listing, its photos, description, price and the phone number you give. The phone number is shown to another signed-in person only when they deliberately reveal it. We record that a reveal happened (who, which listing, when) to limit abuse, and tell the seller only how many reveals there were.
  • Payments. A listing fee is paid through Apple in-app purchase. We receive Apple's verified transaction record (transaction ID, product, dates). We never see your card details.
  • Notifications. If you turn on push notifications, a device token for your phone.
  • Support messages. What you write in Help and feedback. If you tick "Attach basic app information" we add the app version, platform and mode, and nothing else.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location. Weather uses a city you pick by hand. The app does not ask for GPS.
  • No contacts, health data, browsing history or search history.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: sign you in, keep your wardrobe, show your friends what you chose to share, run listings and verify the listing fee, send notifications you turned on, and answer support requests.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and in-app purchase. Google provides Sign in with Google if you choose it.
  • Open-Meteo receives only the coordinates of the city you pick, to return the weather.
  • If cloud photo cutouts are ever switched on, a photo you choose to scan is sent to Photoroom for background removal, and only after you give separate consent.

We do not sell your data.

Keeping and deleting it

You can export your data and delete your account from Settings. Deleting removes your wardrobe, photos, plans, profile and sign-in. Anonymised records of listing-fee payments (Apple transaction ID, product and dates) are kept for financial and legal obligations for a fixed retention period; they are no longer linked to you.

Children

Shiyaka is for people aged 12 and over. If you believe a younger person has an account, tell us through Help and feedback and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can edit or delete anything you added, turn notifications off, block or report people, and request your data or deletion from the app. If you live somewhere with data-protection rights, you can use them by asking through Help and feedback.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it applies. Questions: use Help and feedback in the app (Settings, then Support), or email ahmednoeman04@gmail.com.
